One of the big things pre-surgery that was on my mind was what my healing would look like. I couldn't imagine it, and as I talked about in *Lump*, the consultant didn't give me much of an idea. At one point I got offered the phrase “wound healing is generally about six weeks” and as soon as I had that I stopped worrying about it. As it turned out it has been closer to **twenty** weeks, but even before a month was up I'd found my patience with being a patient.

Healing was slow. SLOW slow. For the first 12 weeks I had a negative pressure/vacuum dressing which slurped at my wound and pulled in all of the edges. Eventually I said goodbye to Mr Fartbox and hello to a series of conventional dressings that weren't fond of the distinctly concave shape of my back. Of course, now in my final week (??!) of wearing a dressing have we found the perfect size and style of dressing. Shout out to Cutimed, you're great for my hole.

**this wasn’t even my full pharmacy haul**

What I have now is a glorified plaster. At my last dressing change my fab nurse at the GP shocked me with the news that I'd only need the GP appointments for another week. I looked baffled at her — “but then what do I do?”  
“Nothing.”

I spent the rest of the day in quite a strange mood. I wasn't expecting to have so many emotions about coming to the end of this stage of my healing. The “open wound” era was the aspect I was most nervous about, the part that made me feel most vulnerable, but pretty soon I let myself into the hands of the wonderful nurses at Gillies Clinic at Nottingham City Hospital and then eventually my local GP. I did what I was told, I made sure the dressing behaved in between appointments and I let myself be patient.

First the vac pressure came down. I was suddenly freshly nervous — what do I do next? Am I okay to do this? Do that? How do I shower? Then I gradually settled and the weeks ticked by. Then discussions of the vac started up. Nervous again. But it's doing its thing? What will happen? Will it hurt more? Then the vac came off and I did a little spin. Not long after I stopped going to the clinic and started going to my GP. Another step on the road and suddenly I'm nervous again. The dressings are different. The nurses don't know me yet. But that turned out to be fine as well. They know me and my hole well now and soon I won't be a regular face in the surgery.

Even though I know it's all fine, this final step on the healing journey is filling my nerves yet again. Every month has been a new new normal and every month has been fine. I just wish the monkey brain remembered that.
